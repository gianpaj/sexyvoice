Choose an AI character and talk in real time. Each character combines a prompt
that defines its personality with a voice that determines how it sounds.

## Before you start

- Use a working microphone and allow microphone access in your browser.
- Keep at least 1,000 credits in your balance to start a call.
- Free users have a total allowance of five minutes across all calls. A top-up
  or subscription payment removes that limit and unlocks custom characters,
  saved voices, and scenes. Calls still use credits.

## Start a call

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a character">
    Pick a featured character, or one from **Your characters**. Featured
    characters are predefined personalities supplied by SexyVoice.ai. Use
    **Listen** to hear a sample.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your call settings">
    Open the character to choose a language and an optional scene. A scene adds
    a situation for this call, separately from the saved character. Check that
    your microphone is ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start talking">
    Click **Start call**. You can also use **Call** on a character card to start
    directly. Speak naturally, interrupt, or change topics as you would in a
    conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="End the call">
    Click **End call**. The ended screen lets you call again, edit your custom
    character, or return to the character list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create your own character

Paid users can create up to 10 custom characters. Give your character a name,
describe its personality, and select a built-in or saved cloned voice. You can
start the personality prompt from an example, such as a tutor or interview
coach. Save the character before calling.

To clone a voice in the creator, choose **Clone a voice**, record or upload at
least 10 seconds of speech, name it, and confirm that you have permission to use
it. Saving the voice is free. See [Voice Cloning](./voice-cloning) for reuse and
deletion details.

You can edit or duplicate your custom characters. **Make your own version** of
a featured character copies its name, description, and voice; you supply your
own personality prompt.

## During a call

- Mute or unmute your microphone.
- Open **Audio settings** to choose an input device or adjust the noise filter.
- Watch the character's listening, thinking, or talking status and the call timer.

Set the character, voice, language, and scene before starting. To change them,
end the call and adjust the settings before calling again.

## Credits

Calls cost 1,000 credits per minute, whether you use a built-in or cloned voice.
The timer starts when the character is ready to talk, and your credit balance
refreshes after the call ends.
