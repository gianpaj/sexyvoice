Create a custom AI voice from a short audio sample. Generate speech right away,
or save your voice to use again for voice generation and live calls.

Only clone your own voice or a voice you have permission to use.

## Before you start

- Record or upload at least 10 seconds of one person speaking clearly.
- Upload an MP3, WAV, OGG, Opus, M4A, or WebM file, up to 4.5 MB.
- Use a quiet recording without music or background voices. Longer clips are
  trimmed, so the first part should contain clear speech.
- Saving a voice requires a top-up or subscription payment. Generating audio
  requires enough credits in your balance.

## Generate speech from a recording

<Steps>
  <Step title="Provide reference audio">
    On the Clone page, upload a file or record with your microphone. Reference
    audio is the recording of the voice you want to reproduce.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a language and enter text">
    Choose the language for the generated speech. Enter the words you want the
    voice to say. The form shows the text limit for your language and account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate audio">
    Confirm that you have permission to use the voice, then click **Generate
    Audio**. You can also press `Cmd+Enter` on Mac or `Ctrl+Enter` on Windows.
    Preview and download the result when it is ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Generating from a recording creates an audio file. To keep a voice for later,
use the save option below.

## Save a voice for later

Turn on **Save a voice for calls**, provide a recording, enter a name, and
confirm permission to save the voice. Click **Save voice**. You can also clone
and save a voice while creating a [character for calls](./voice-call).

You can keep up to 10 saved voices. They are private to your account, and the
voice provider retains the reference sample so you can reuse them.

- For speech generation, select a saved voice above the Clone form and enter
  text. Select it again to return to recording or uploading reference audio.
- For calls, choose the saved voice when creating or editing a character. A
  character combines a personality prompt with a voice.
- To delete a saved voice, use its Delete action on the Clone page. Generated
  audio stays in History. Characters using that voice need a replacement before
  their next call.

## Text limits

| Generation method         | Text limit                                            |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Recording with Voxtral    | 1,000 characters for free users; 4,000 for paid users |
| Recording with Chatterbox | 300 characters                                        |
| Saved Inworld voice       | 2,000 characters                                      |
| Saved Mistral voice       | 4,000 characters                                      |

The language determines which model handles generation from a recording.
Inworld saved voices can generate speech in the language you select. Mistral
saved voices use the language they were saved with.

The save mode shows languages supported by the configured voice provider.
Reference audio is trimmed to the first 30 seconds for saved Inworld voices,
25 seconds for Mistral, or 10 seconds for Chatterbox when audio conversion permits.

## Audio cleanup and credits

Saving a voice is free for paid users. Generating speech uses credits based on
the text, and calls use the [live-call rate](./voice-call#credits). All use your
existing credit balance.

When generating from a recording, optional **Clean up the reference audio**
costs 10 credits per second of trimmed reference audio when cleanup succeeds.
When saving an Inworld voice, that option removes background noise for free.
Use it for noisy recordings; it can make clean audio sound worse. It is not
offered when saving a Mistral voice.
