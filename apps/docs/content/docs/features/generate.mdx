Generate lets you turn any text into lifelike audio using AI voices. Choose from a variety of voices, enter your text, and download the audio file.

## How to Generate Speech

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a voice">
    Choose a voice from the dropdown. Voices are grouped by language. Click the
    play button to preview how each voice sounds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your text">
    Type or paste the text you want to convert. Character limits depend on the
    voice model (see table below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click **Generate Audio** or press `Cmd+Enter` (Mac) / `Ctrl+Enter`
    (Windows). Wait for processing to complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    Preview the audio in the player, then click **Download** to save the file.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Voice models

The available public catalog has 41 voices: 35 text-to-speech voices across two voice
families and 6 built-in voices for [AI calls](./voice-call).
The text limits below apply to the dashboard; the form shows the limit for your
selected voice. For API limits, see the [API quickstart](../guides/api).

| Voice model           | Character Limit      | Output Formats | Special Features                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------- | -------------------- | -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Gemini 3.8 voices** | 500 free; 1,000 paid | WAV            | Speech style instructions plus inline emotion tags like `<sigh>` and `<short pause>`; includes Spanish voices for Spain and Mexico |
| **Grok voices**       | 500 free; 1,000 paid | MP3, WAV       | Multi-language support with expressive tags                                                                                        |

<Callout>
  Gemini voices show a text box where you can describe how you want the voice to
  sound (e.g., "warm and friendly" or "serious and professional"). Choose a
  Gemini 3.8 voice, such as `kore`, `zephyr`, or a Spanish voice for Spain or
  Mexico.
</Callout>

<Callout>
  Grok voices support MP3 and WAV output. Add inline tags such as `[laugh]`,
  `[sigh]`, or `[pause]` for expression. Wrapper tags change delivery over a
  passage. See the [Grok speech tags guide](../guides/grok-speech-tags).
</Callout>

## Emotion tags for Gemini 3.8 voices

Gemini 3.8 voices accept inline emotion tags in any supported speech language. Keep the tags in English.

`<argh>`, `<breath>`, `<heavy breath>`, `<exhales>`, `<cackle>`, `<cheer>`, `<chuckle>`, `<chuckles>`, `<cough>`, `<cry>`, `<gasp>`, `<giggle>`, `<groan>`, `<growl>`, `<grunt>`, `<grr>`, `<hiss>`, `<laugh>`, `<laughter>`, `<moan>`, `<pant>`, `<pff>`, `<phew>`, `<scream>`, `<shout>`, `<shriek>`, `<sigh>`, `<sighs>`, `<sneeze>`, `<snicker>`, `<snort>`, `<sob>`, `<throat-clearing>`, `<tsk>`, `<whimper>`, `<whispers>`, `<whispering>`, `<yawn>`, `<short pause>`, `<long pause>`

**Example:** `Ven aquí. <short pause> <laughter> No me lo puedo creer.`

## Credits

Credit charges depend on your text, voice model, and account. Use **Estimate
credits** in the generator before generating audio. Gemini charges depend on
token usage; Grok charges in 100-character blocks of 100 credits.

The dashboard and external API use the same credit balance. Your remaining
credits are shown in the dashboard.
