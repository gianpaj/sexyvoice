Generate lets you turn any text into lifelike audio using AI voices. Choose from a variety of voices, enter your text, and download the audio file.

## How to Generate Speech

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a voice">
    Choose a voice from the dropdown. Voices are grouped by language. Click the play button to preview how each voice sounds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your text">
    Type or paste the text you want to convert. Character limits depend on the voice type (see table below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click **Generate** or press `Cmd+Enter` (Mac) / `Ctrl+Enter` (Windows). Wait for processing to complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    Preview the audio in the player, then click **Download** to save the file.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Voice Types

The catalog has 57 public text-to-speech voices across four voice families.

| Voice Type | Character Limit | Output Formats | Special Features |
|------------|-----------------|----------------|------------------|
| **Gemini 3.8 voices** | 1,000 characters | WAV | Style instructions plus inline vocal tags like `<sigh>` and `<short pause>`; includes Spanish voices for Spain and Mexico |
| **Gemini 3.1 voices** | 1,000 characters | WAV | Style instructions plus inline expression tags like `[whispering]` and `[pause]` |
| **Gemini 2.5 voices** | 1,000 characters | WAV | Style instructions for tone/emotion |
| **Grok voices** | 1,000 characters | MP3, WAV | Multi-language support with expressive tags |

<Callout>
Gemini voices show a text box where you can describe how you want the voice to sound (e.g., "warm and friendly" or "serious and professional"). The same voice name, such as `kore` or `zephyr`, is available on Gemini 2.5, 3.1, and 3.8, so pick the model version you prefer.
</Callout>

<Callout>
Grok voices support both MP3 and WAV output. They also support expressive tags like `[laugh]`, `[sigh]`, `[pause]`, and wrapper tags like `<whisper>...</whisper>`, `<soft>...</soft>` for fine-grained control over delivery.
</Callout>

## Emotion Tags (Gemini 3.8 voices)

Gemini 3.8 voices accept inline vocal tags in any transcript language. Keep the tags in English.

`<argh>`, `<breath>`, `<heavy breath>`, `<exhales>`, `<cackle>`, `<cheer>`, `<chuckle>`, `<chuckles>`, `<cough>`, `<cry>`, `<gasp>`, `<giggle>`, `<groan>`, `<growl>`, `<grunt>`, `<grr>`, `<hiss>`, `<laugh>`, `<laughter>`, `<moan>`, `<pant>`, `<pff>`, `<phew>`, `<scream>`, `<shout>`, `<shriek>`, `<sigh>`, `<sighs>`, `<sneeze>`, `<snicker>`, `<snort>`, `<sob>`, `<throat-clearing>`, `<tsk>`, `<whimper>`, `<whispers>`, `<whispering>`, `<yawn>`, `<short pause>`, `<long pause>`

**Example:** `Ven aquí. <short pause> <laughter> No me lo puedo creer.`

## Credits

Credits are calculated based on text length and voice type:

- **Gemini voices**: ~1 credit per token (based on text and audio length)
- **Grok voices**: 4 credits per 100 characters

You need at least **10 credits** to generate audio. Your remaining credits are shown at the top of the page.
